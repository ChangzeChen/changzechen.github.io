Research Experience and Project

Deep Learning and PDE based Early Warning System for African Desert Locust
Undergraduate Independent Research, sponsored by Wangdao Project, Fudan Undergraduate Research Opportunity Program(FDUROP)
Advisors: Yong Wang (Fudan University) and Yilun Han (UC San Diego)
I applied CNNs and time-series analysis to investigate relationships between meteorological conditions and locust population density, and developed PDE-based numerical models to simulate population-density evolution and estimate the risk of progression toward large-scale locust outbreaks.

Matrix-Free Input–Output Analysis of Wall-Bounded Flows
Research Assistant
Advisors: Chang Liu (University of Connecticut)
I worked in the FLUids, rEduction, Nonlinearity, and Turbulence (FLUENT) Lab and developed matrix-free input–output analysis for linearized Navier–Stokes equations using Fourier and Chebyshev discretization and iterative linear solvers. Applied SVD and adjoint-based methods to compute dominant forcing–response modes and characterize disturbance amplification in wall-bounded flows.

Seminar of Real Analysis: Hausdorff Measure and Hausdorff Dimension
I gave a presentation of the construction of Hausdorff measure and proved that $\mathcal H^\alpha$ defines a metric outer measure on $\mathbb R$, with all Borel sets Carathéodory measurable. Also proved that the one-dimensional Hausdorff measure $\mathcal H^1$ coincides with Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb R$. You can find my note here.
