Seminar of Real Analysis: Hausdorff Measure and Hausdorff Dimension

I gave a presentation on the construction of Hausdorff measure and proved that $\mathcal H^\alpha$ defines a metric outer measure, with all Borel sets Carathéodory measurable.

I also proved that the one-dimensional Hausdorff measure $\mathcal H^1$ coincides with Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb R$.
