Research Experience

Matrix-Free Input–Output Analysis of Wall-Bounded Flows

Research Assistant
FLUids, rEduction, Nonlinearity, and Turbulence (FLUENT) Lab
University of Connecticut

Advisor: Prof. Chang Liu

I developed matrix-free input–output analysis for linearized Navier–Stokes equations using Fourier and Chebyshev discretization and iterative linear solvers. I applied SVD and adjoint-based methods to compute dominant forcing–response modes and characterize disturbance amplification in wall-bounded flows.

Deep Learning and PDE-Based Early Warning System for African Desert Locust

Undergraduate Independent Research
Sponsored by the Wangdao Project, Fudan Undergraduate Research Opportunity Program (FDUROP)

Advisors: Prof. Yong Wang (Fudan University) and Yilun Han (UC San Diego)

I applied convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and time-series analysis to investigate relationships between meteorological conditions and desert locust population density.

I also developed PDE-based numerical models to simulate population-density evolution and estimate the risk of progression toward large-scale locust outbreaks.
